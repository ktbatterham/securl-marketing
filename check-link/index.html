Check where a link goes before you click it

Paste a shortened, unfamiliar or suspicious public link to reveal its redirect path and final destination without opening the destination page in your browser.

SecURL follows bounded public redirects from its server and reports each observed hop, hostname change, HTTPS downgrade, unusual port and final HTTP response. It does not run page scripts, download files, submit forms or sign in to any service.

The result is evidence, not a promise that a link is safe. A page can change after a check, hide behaviour behind a login or serve different content to another visitor. Always confirm that the final hostname and context match what you expected.
